Technical · BIDNEX

Bidnex

Bid your confidence on technical questions, then out-prompt AI to recreate a hidden scenario.

Rounds
Two
Start Points
100
Round 2 Time
3 Min
Category
Tech

Rounds

Round 01 — Technical Bidding

Technical Bidding

Teams start with 100 points and bid based on confidence. The highest bidder gets the first chance to answer. Correct answers gain points; wrong answers lose points and pass the chance to the next bidder.

Round 02 — Prompting

Prompting

Teams get 3 minutes to understand an idiom-based scenario. The scenario is then removed, and teams create an AI-generated image using their own prompt. Judges compare the image with the original scenario and intended meaning.
