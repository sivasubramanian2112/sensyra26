What is
Sensyra

Connect · Innovate · Empower

What it is
A one-day technical and non-technical symposium hosted by the Department of CSE (IoT).
Organised by
K.L.N. College of Engineering, Department of Computer Science & Engineering (IoT).
When
14 Oct 2026, 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM — registration through to prize announcements. Online registration closes 12 Oct 2026.
Where
Pottapolayam, Sivagangai District, Tamil Nadu — near Madurai. Full address and map are in Contact.
Events
Three technical, three non-technical — Paper Presentation, IoTronics and BIDNEX; IPL Auction, CINETRIX and ARTiverse.
Entry
₹200 single pass. Open to B.E., B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc (CS/IT), MCA students, and other interested participants.
Getting there
Free college transport is available — see the transport schedule.

Event
Descriptions

Teams of 2–4 may take part in one technical and one non-technical event.

Technical
Paper Presentation

THESORA — present original research across 13 tracks in a 7-minute PPT slot, teams of 1–3.
IoTronics

Two rounds — the Connect the Quiz challenge, then a live IoT Build Battle.
BIDNEX

Bid points on technical questions, then out-prompt AI in an idiom-scenario challenge.
Non-Technical
IPL Auction

Clear the cricket quiz, crack the Top 8, then build your squad at the auction table.
CINETRIX

Tune Trigger, Cine Connect, and a surprise round revealed at the event.
ARTiverse

Panic Pictionary — 60 seconds, one drawing, unlimited guesses.

Questions,
Answered

Who can join SENSYRA ’26 events?

Students from B.E., B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc (CS/IT), MCA, and candidates from other domains with an interest in participating are welcome to join.

What is the process to sign up for competitions?

Simply click the Register Now button, which will redirect you to our official Google Form. Complete the form to confirm your registration. Register here ↗

Am I allowed to take part in both technical and cultural events?

Teams consisting of 2–4 members may participate in one technical event and one non-technical event.

Will certificates be issued for all participants and winners?

Yes. All participants will receive a certificate of participation, and winners will be awarded special certificates in recognition of their achievement.

Is there any travel assistance provided to reach KLN College of Engineering?

Yes. College transport facilities are available free of charge. For further details, visit: Transport schedule ↗

How are the registration charges structured for different events?

A single universal fee of ₹200 allows you to participate in events across all categories.
